Privacy Policy

ICU Drip Trainer · Effective September 15, 2026

ICU Drip Trainer is designed to work locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not collect or sell personal information.

Information the app collects

ICU Drip Trainer does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, contacts, photos, precise location, health information, advertising identifiers, or payment information. It does not use analytics, advertising, social sign-in, or crash reporting services.

Information stored on your device

When you answer quiz questions, the app stores your quiz results and timestamps in local device storage so that progress and accuracy can be shown later. This information is not sent to the developer or to a remote server by the app.

You can remove this quiz history using the reset option in the Progress screen. Uninstalling the app also normally removes its local storage, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Information shared with third parties

The app does not share the locally stored quiz history with third parties. ICU Drip Trainer does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party accounts.

Your operating system, app store, or device manufacturer may process standard information independently of this app, such as information needed to install, update, secure, or diagnose applications. Those activities are governed by the applicable provider's privacy policy, not by ICU Drip Trainer.

Internet and offline use

The educational content and quiz progress features are packaged with the app and are intended to work without an internet connection. The app does not require a developer-hosted server, Replit service, or external API for its core features.

Children's privacy

ICU Drip Trainer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe personal information was provided to the developer in connection with the app, please contact the developer through the support contact shown on the app's store listing.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact published with the ICU Drip Trainer store listing.

Educational disclaimer

ICU Drip Trainer is an educational study aid. It is not medical advice, does not replace clinical judgment, and should not be used as the sole basis for patient care or medication decisions.
